A shared record for relief supplies

From a donor’s hands to a patient’s bedside, every handoff is written down.

MedTrace logs medical donations on Ethereum as they move between donors, NGOs and clinics. Nobody has to take anyone’s word for where a shipment went, because every stop is signed.

01

Sign the register

Before you can send or receive anything, tell the network who you are. Pick one role per wallet:

  1. DonorOpens a shipment and names who it’s for.
  2. NGOReceives stock, moves it on, passes it to partners.
  3. Healthcare facilityThe last stop. Confirms supplies reached patients.
Heads up: each MetaMask account can register once. To add an NGO or clinic, switch to (or create) another account in MetaMask first.
Choose a role to see how it participates in the donation flow.
